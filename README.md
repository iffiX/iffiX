I am a CS PhD student at Northwestern University, advised by [Sam Kriegman](https://skriegman.github.io/). I build computational systems for creating, representing, learning, and evolving complex embodied structures. My current research explores scalable physical worlds in which bodies, perception, behavior, and environments can evolve together.
